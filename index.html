⚠️ Avoiding fake recruiters / scam listings

  • Genuine hospital recruiters use a company email domain (e.g. @hcahealthcare.com), never Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook for official offers.
  • No legitimate employer ever asks you to pay for training, equipment, or "processing fees" before you're hired.
  • Be wary of postings with vague hospital names, no address, or that push you to chat entirely off-platform (WhatsApp/Telegram) within the first message.
  • Verify the recruiter's LinkedIn: real recruiters usually have a full work history, mutual connections, and are listed on the hospital's own careers page or staff directory.
  • Confirm a posting is real by searching the hospital's own official career site for the same role before applying.
  • Never share your SSN, bank details, or passport/visa documents until you have a signed, verifiable offer from the hospital's official HR system.
